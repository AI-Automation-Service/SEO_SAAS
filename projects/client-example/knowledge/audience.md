# Target Audience

## Primary Persona

**Name:** Home Hannah  
**Age:** 30–45  
**Location:** United States (suburban)  
**Income:** Middle to upper-middle class  

## Goals

- Find reliable, high-quality products
- Research before buying
- Get clear instructions and support

## Pain Points

- Overwhelmed by too many options
- Worried about product quality
- Wants fast shipping and easy returns

## Where They Spend Time Online

- Google search
- YouTube (product reviews)
- Reddit (community recommendations)
- Pinterest (inspiration)

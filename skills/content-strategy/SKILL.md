Write in structured Markdown. Be specific — name actual topics, formats, and priorities. Reference the real keyword and business data you are given.

## Topic Cluster Model

Organize all content around pillar pages and cluster articles:

**Pillar page (hub):**
- Broad topic, 2,000–5,000+ words, comprehensive guide
- Targets the primary keyword for that topic
- Links to ALL cluster articles in that topic
- The hub page in the keyword data maps to this pillar

**Cluster articles (spokes):**
- 6–12 articles per pillar (minimum 6 for topical authority)
- Each covers one focused subtopic, 800–2,500 words
- Each links BACK to the pillar page
- Related spokes should cross-link to each other

**Why it works:**
- Topical authority: comprehensive coverage signals expertise to search engines
- Internal linking: clear hub-and-spoke structure passes link equity to the pillar
- Cannibalization prevention: one page per topic/intent
- AI citations: clustered content receives ~42% more AI citations than isolated pages

## Content Type Selection

Match content type to search intent and funnel stage:

| Intent | Funnel stage | Best content type |
|---|---|---|
| Informational | TOFU | How-to guide, FAQ, glossary, explainer |
| Commercial | MOFU | Comparison, review, "best X" list, case study |
| Transactional | BOFU | Service page, landing page, pricing page |
| Navigational | — | Brand page, product page |

**Evergreen vs timely mix:**
- 70–75% evergreen: pillar guides, how-tos, comparisons, glossaries. Drives long-term traffic.
- 25–30% timely: seasonal, trending, news. Quick spikes, needs linking into evergreen pillars.

## Content Briefs

For each priority content piece, specify:
- **Target keyword** and secondary keywords
- **Content type** (guide, comparison, FAQ, etc.)
- **Word count target** (based on SERP competitor analysis)
- **Key H2 headings** to cover
- **Internal links** to add (which hub it links to + related spokes)
- **E-E-A-T signals** to include (first-hand experience, specific data, credentials)
- **Priority** (high/medium/low) and estimated traffic potential

For YMYL topics (health, finance, legal), plan author credentials and citation structure before any writing begins.

## Writing Style Principles

When giving content direction:
- **Benefits over features**: Connect what a feature does to what it means for the user
- **One idea per section**: Each H2 should advance one argument

For voice, active voice, and avoiding vague language, apply the standards in `writing-rules.md` (loaded automatically).

## Publishing Cadence Recommendation

Base cadence on the cluster count and content capacity:

| Content depth | Recommended frequency |
|---|---|
| 3–5 clusters | 1–2 posts/week per cluster until pillar + 6 spokes exist |
| 6–10 clusters | 2–3 posts/week; prioritize top 3 clusters first |
| 10+ clusters | Focus on depth over breadth; complete one cluster before starting the next |
